Answers to common questions about consulting our doctors and visiting our hospital branches.

Women are encouraged to consult a gynaecologist for routine annual preventive checkups and promptly whenever experiencing symptoms such as:

  • Irregular, missed, prolonged, or abnormally heavy menstrual periods.
  • Severe lower pelvic pain, cramps, or discomfort during intercourse.
  • Symptoms of Polycystic Ovary Syndrome (PCOS) like unwanted facial hair, acne, or weight struggles.
  • Pre-conception planning, fertility advice, and confirmation of pregnancy.
  • Perimenopausal and menopausal symptoms including hot flushes, mood changes, and bone density concerns.

A consultation with Dr. Vijaya Lakshmi is conducted in complete privacy, dignity, and comfort. The doctor reviews your complete medical and menstrual history, listens attentively to your symptoms, and performs a gentle, clinical examination only when necessary. Recommended diagnostic tests (such as pelvic ultrasound or hormone assays) and treatment options are clearly explained with no rush.

Antenatal (prenatal) care is critical for monitoring maternal health, fetal growth, and preventing potential complications such as gestational diabetes, hypertension (preeclampsia), and preterm labour. A standard pregnancy checkup schedule typically involves:

  • Weeks 4 to 28: Monthly visits (ultrasound dating scan, blood profiling, nutritional supplementation).
  • Weeks 28 to 36: Fortnightly (every 2 weeks) visits to monitor fetal growth and blood pressure.
  • Weeks 36 to Delivery: Weekly checkups to assess baby position, cervical readiness, and birth planning.

Normal vaginal delivery is the natural childbirth process where the baby is delivered through the birth canal, typically offering faster maternal recovery and shorter hospital stays. Lower Segment Caesarean Section (LSCS) is a surgical procedure where the obstetrician delivers the baby through a precise incision in the mother's abdomen and uterus. At Sri Vijaya Nursing Home, we prioritize normal physiological delivery whenever safe, with round-the-clock surgical readiness if an emergency C-section is required.

LSCS (Lower Segment Caesarean Section) is a life-saving obstetric surgical delivery performed when natural birth presents risks to the mother or baby. Common clinical indications include fetal distress, abnormal baby presentation (breech or transverse), placenta praevia, cephalopelvic disproportion, previous uterine surgeries, or failure of labor progression. Our operation theater is equipped for both planned elective LSCS and rapid emergency C-sections.

Laparoscopic tubectomy is a minimally invasive keyhole surgical procedure for permanent female contraception (sterilization). Using a miniature camera and fine surgical instruments inserted through tiny abdominal incisions, the fallopian tubes are blocked or clipped to prevent fertilization. Benefits include minimal pain, tiny scars, low infection risk, and same-day or next-day discharge.

Laparoscopic hysterectomy is the surgical removal of the uterus performed through small keyhole incisions using laparoscopic technology. It is recommended for conditions such as large symptomatic uterine fibroids, severe endometriosis, chronic abnormal uterine bleeding, or pelvic organ prolapse. Patients experience significantly less post-operative pain, minimal blood loss, and a much faster return to routine life compared to conventional open abdominal hysterectomy.
