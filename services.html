Specialized Gynaecology

Ovarian Problems & Ovarian Cysts

Ovarian disorders are among the most common causes of pelvic discomfort, hormonal irregularities, and fertility challenges in women of all reproductive ages. At Sri Vijaya Nursing Home, Dr. Vijaya Lakshmi provides evidence-based medical treatment and minimally invasive laparoscopic interventions for all ovarian conditions.

Conditions We Treat

  • Follicular & Corpus Luteum Cysts: Functional cysts requiring routine sonographic monitoring or medical resolution.
  • Dermoid Cysts (Cystic Teratomas): Benign ovarian tumors treated with organ-preserving laparoscopic cystectomy.
  • Endometriomas ("Chocolate Cysts"): Ovarian endometriosis causing severe dysmenorrhea, pelvic pain, and infertility.
  • Polycystic Ovary Syndrome (PCOS / PCOD): Comprehensive hormonal rebalancing, weight guidance, and ovulation induction.
  • Ovarian Torsion Emergencies: Prompt clinical diagnosis and emergency surgical intervention to preserve the ovary.

Our Treatment Approach

We emphasize ovary-sparing laparoscopic surgery wherever possible to protect your ovarian reserve and future fertility. With high-definition laparoscopy, patients experience minimal incisions, reduced post-operative discomfort, and rapid recovery.

Preventive Oncology

Women's Cancer Screening & Early Detection

Cervical and breast cancers are highly treatable and curable when detected in early or pre-cancerous stages. Sri Vijaya Nursing Home offers comprehensive, gentle, and confidential screening services utilizing world-class diagnostic modalities.

🧪

Pap Smear Test

Quick, painless cervical swab to detect abnormal pre-cancerous cellular changes years before cancer develops. Liquid-based cytology ensures high diagnostic accuracy.

🔬

Colposcopy Examination

Advanced microscopic examination of the cervix using a specialized colposcope to illuminate and magnify tissues showing suspicious or borderline Pap smear findings.

📋

Cervical Biopsy

Targeted small tissue sampling from suspicious cervical zones during colposcopy for definitive histopathology verification and treatment staging.

🎗️

Mammogram & Breast Screening

Low-dose X-ray breast screening and clinical palpation to identify early breast lumps, microcalcifications, and benign fibroadenomas.

Maternal-Fetal Medicine

Fetal Growth & Advanced Ultrasound Diagnostics

Every pregnancy journey requires attentive, scientific monitoring to ensure baby is developing healthily at every gestational week. Our fetal surveillance protocol safeguards both mother and baby from silent intrauterine risks.

Fetal Growth Surveillance Features

  • Serial Growth Scans: Continuous tracking of biparietal diameter (BPD), head circumference (HC), abdominal circumference (AC), femur length (FL), and estimated fetal weight (EFW).
  • Color Doppler Sonography: Evaluating blood flow resistance across the umbilical artery, uterine arteries, and fetal middle cerebral artery (MCA) to detect placental insufficiency or IUGR early.
  • Amniotic Fluid Index (AFI) Assessment: Timely identification and therapeutic management of Oligohydramnios (low fluid) and Polyhydramnios (excess fluid).
  • Non-Stress Test (NST) & Biophysical Profile: Electronic fetal heart rate monitoring assessing fetal movements, tone, and oxygenation.

Reproductive Health & Infertility

Fertility Care, Uterine Cleansing & IVF Lab Assistance

Infertility can be an emotional journey, but with empathetic guidance and modern reproductive medicine, parenthood is achievable. Sri Vijaya Nursing Home offers comprehensive infertility evaluations, uterine preparation protocols, and assisted reproductive support.

Fertility Services & Procedures

  • Fertility Cleaning & Uterine Cavity Preparation: Diagnostic saline sonography, gentle uterine cavity wash, and hysteroscopic removal of polyps, septums, or adhesions to create an optimal endometrial lining for embryo implantation.
  • Ovulation Induction & Follicular Tracking: Scientific medication protocols accompanied by serial ultrasound monitoring to identify peak fertile days.
  • Tubal Patency Testing: Hysterosalpingography (HSG) and diagnostic laparoscopy to verify fallopian tube health and openness.
  • IVF Lab Coordination & IUI: Comprehensive pre-IVF evaluation, semen analysis, partner counseling, and protocol management in partnership with certified IVF laboratories.

Diagnostic Pathology & Imaging

Ultrasound Imaging & Diagnostic Histopathology (Histology)

Accurate clinical decisions depend on precise diagnostic imaging and laboratory tissue analysis. Sri Vijaya Nursing Home provides dedicated pelvic sonography and histopathology / histology evaluation for rapid, dependable diagnoses.

Diagnostic Capabilities

  • Pelvic & Transvaginal Ultrasound (TVS): High-resolution evaluation of uterine fibroids, endometrial thickness, adenomyosis, and ovarian morphology.
  • Obstetric Ultrasound: Dating scans, NT scans, anomaly scans, and biophysical profile.
  • Histopathology & Histology Services: Microscopic cellular examination of endometrial biopsy tissue, cervical biopsy specimens, and resected cyst walls to definitively rule out malignancy and guide medical therapy.

Surgical Procedures

Surgical Services

Sri Vijaya Nursing Home provides selected surgical services, including both open and minimally invasive procedures. Surgical decisions are made based on thorough clinical evaluation, and our team ensures patients understand the process, benefits, and risks.

LSCS (Lower Segment Caesarean Section)

LSCS is a surgical delivery method performed when natural delivery is not advisable or safe for the mother or baby. Our surgical team follows established protocols to ensure patient safety during and after the procedure.

Laparoscopic Tubectomy

Laparoscopic tubectomy is a minimally invasive surgical procedure for permanent contraception. It involves small incisions and generally requires a shorter recovery period compared to open surgery. Patients should consult with the doctor to discuss suitability, risks, and recovery expectations.

Laparoscopic Hysterectomy

Laparoscopic hysterectomy is a minimally invasive procedure for the removal of the uterus. The decision to perform this surgery depends on the individual clinical situation and the doctor's assessment. Our team provides thorough pre-operative counselling and post-operative care.
