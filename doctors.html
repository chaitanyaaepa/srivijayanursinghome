Institutional Excellence

8 Years of Clinical Leadership at Singareni Main Hospital, Kothagudem

Prior to leading clinical care at Sri Vijaya Nursing Home, Dr. Vijaya Lakshmi served for 8 years at Singareni Main Hospital in Kothagudem. At this premier multi-speciality tertiary care hospital serving the Singareni mining community, she managed heavy-volume obstetric emergencies, thousands of normal deliveries, complicated Lower Segment Caesarean Sections (LSCS), and advanced surgical gynaecological procedures.

About Dr. Vijaya Lakshmi

Dr. Vijaya Lakshmi is the lead consultant gynaecologist and obstetrician at Sri Vijaya Nursing Home, conducting specialized outpatient clinics and managing surgical maternity admissions across both Palvancha and Kothagudem. Her clinical approach combines decades of rigorous hospital-based obstetrics with deeply compassionate, patient-first care.

Under her stewardship, patients have access to round-the-clock maternal vigilance, comprehensive antenatal screening, painless labour support, neonatal stabilization coordination, laparoscopic tubectomy, laparoscopic hysterectomy, and specialized treatments for adolescent gynaecology and PCOS.

Patients seeking a consultation with Dr. Vijaya Lakshmi can contact our Palvancha clinic at 90632 76907 to confirm consultation timings and appointment slots.
