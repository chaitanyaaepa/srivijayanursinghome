About Sri Vijaya Nursing Home

Learn about our healthcare facility, mission, and the values that guide our commitment to patient care in Kothagudem and Palvancha.

Our Healthcare Facility

Sri Vijaya Nursing Home is a healthcare facility serving patients and families across Bhadradri Kothagudem District in Telangana, India. With branches in both Kothagudem and Palvancha, we aim to make quality healthcare more accessible to communities in this region.

Our Palvancha branch places particular emphasis on gynaecology and women's healthcare, offering consultations and procedures under the care of experienced medical professionals. The Kothagudem branch serves as our main facility, providing healthcare services to the local community.

We believe that every patient deserves to be treated with dignity, empathy, and clear communication. Our team strives to create an environment where patients feel heard, informed, and supported throughout their healthcare journey.

Our Foundation

Mission & Values

The principles that guide how we care for our patients and serve our community.

Compassionate Care

We approach every patient interaction with empathy, kindness, and genuine concern for their well-being and comfort.

Patient Dignity

Every patient is treated with respect, privacy, and consideration regardless of their background or circumstances.

Clear Communication

We ensure patients and families understand their care options, enabling informed decisions about treatment.

Responsible Practice

We follow responsible medical practices, ensuring clinical decisions are made based on evidence and in the patient's best interest.

Community Focus

We are rooted in the communities of Kothagudem and Palvancha, striving to improve local access to healthcare services.

Women's Well-Being

Our Palvancha branch is dedicated to women's healthcare, providing specialised gynaecological services with care and discretion.
